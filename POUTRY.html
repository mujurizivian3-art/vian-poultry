📖 About Vian & Family Poultry
Rooted in tradition, growing with innovation.

Vian & Family Poultry started as a small backyard farm in 2005, founded by Vianney Mukasa and his family with just 50 chickens. Today, we are proud to be one of the most trusted poultry enterprises in the region, known for ethical farming and superior bird genetics.


Our mission is to empower local farmers and households with affordable, high-quality poultry products while maintaining animal welfare. We rear layers, broilers, kienyeji (indigenous), and also provide hatchery services. Our family values transparency, integrity, and sustainability — every bird is raised without unnecessary antibiotics, and our feed is locally sourced.
